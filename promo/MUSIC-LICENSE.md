## How it is used

- **Cut:** 64.05–82.05 s of the file (18 s), with a 0.25 s fade-in and a 1.6 s fade-out.
  Mixed under the game's own sound effects and normalised to −14 LUFS.
- **Drop:** the main drop after the long breakdown starts at 72.07 s in the file. It
  lands at 8.02 s in the video, on the 「針織戰甲」 armour-lock chord. `promo/verify.mjs`
  measures the onset in the final mix and in both MP4s.
- **Other cue points:** the pre-drop silence (file 68.95–70.13 s) falls across the scene
  cut at 5.0 s. The stab at 6.09 s in the video brings in the energy caption.
